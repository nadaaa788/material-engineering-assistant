About Material Engineering Assistant

Material Engineering Assistant is a learning project built to help students and engineers explore materials, compare their properties, and diagnose failures through a simple, modern platform.

Our Mission

We aim to make material selection and failure analysis more accessible, combining engineering knowledge with an easy-to-use interface.

What We Offer

- Material Selector: find the best material for your project requirements.
- Failure Diagnosis: identify likely causes of material failure.
- Materials Database: explore metals, polymers, ceramics, and composites.
